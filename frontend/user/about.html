我们是谁

在鲜购拼好货（Freshbuy） 是一家服务于纽约本地社区的生鲜与日用品配送平台， 致力于为周边居民提供 价格透明、配送稳定、沟通直接 的本地购物体验。

我们在做什么

  • 本地采购生鲜、蔬菜、水果、肉禽及高频日用品
  • 通过集中配送、区域拼单等方式降低整体成本
  • 每周固定时间配送，减少浪费，提高效率

为什么选择我们

  • 📍 本地运营：熟悉纽约社区与配送环境
  • 🧾 价格清晰：不玩复杂套路，所见即所得
  • 🚚 稳定配送：固定路线、固定时间，减少不确定性
  • 💬 沟通直接：微信客服，问题可追踪、可反馈

我们的理念

我们相信，社区型购物不应该只是“便宜”， 更重要的是可信任、可持续、能长期服务。 Freshbuy 希望成为你家附近、长期可靠的生活补给站。

Who We Are

Freshbuy is a local grocery and daily essentials delivery service based in New York. We focus on serving nearby communities with transparent pricing, reliable delivery, and direct communication.

What We Do

  • Source fresh produce, meat, and daily essentials locally
  • Reduce costs through scheduled deliveries and regional group orders
  • Deliver at fixed times each week to improve efficiency and reduce waste

Why Choose Freshbuy

  • 📍 Local operation with knowledge of New York neighborhoods
  • 🧾 Clear pricing with no hidden tricks
  • 🚚 Stable delivery routes and schedules
  • 💬 Direct support via WeChat and email

Our Philosophy

We believe local shopping should be more than just low prices. It should be trustworthy, sustainable, and long-term. Freshbuy aims to become a reliable neighborhood supply partner.
